Why these are better build constructs than the old notebook Job:
  - No manual anti-join; Delta's change feed drives incremental processing.
  - Declarative — the engine owns bookkeeping, retries, and checkpointing.
  - Single-file transform; one data-quality constraint attached inline
    (`valid_survey_id EXPECT survey_id IS NOT NULL ON VIOLATION DROP ROW`).
  - Serverless compute; no cluster / environment to maintain.
  - Pipeline + Streaming Table are both first-class UC objects, so SDP
    layer coverage shows up as deployed infrastructure, not just source.
