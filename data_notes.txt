SECTION 1 — VIKING (River Cruises)
================================================================================

1.1 FLEET SIZE
--------------
Viking operates the largest river-cruise fleet in the world. Approximate
counts as of late 2026:

    * Total river vessels in service:  ~80 ships
    * Longships (the signature Rhine/Main/Danube/Seine class, 190 guests):
                                       ~70 of the 80
    * Non-Longship river vessels (Douro, Nile, Mekong, Mississippi, etc.):
                                       ~10

    Fleet is expanding: Viking announced an order for 10 additional
    Longships for delivery across 2025-2026 (five + five), plus continued
    Nile, Douro, and Mekong additions through 2027-2028. By end of 2027
    the river fleet is expected to be ~90 vessels.

1.4 ATTRACTIONS & AMENITIES AVAILABLE ON A VIKING LONGSHIP
-----------------------------------------------------------
A Viking Longship is a 3-deck, 95-cabin, 190-guest vessel built to a shared
Scandinavian-design template (interior architects known for luxury hotels;
accents of slate, teak, and limestone; Nordic textiles and original Nordic
art). Longships are intentionally understated — "the destination is the
attraction." There are no top-deck thrill rides, no casino, no nightclub,
no kids' club. The on-board experience centers on scenic sailing, enrichment,
and regionally-inflected dining. Features available on virtually every
Longship:

   PUBLIC SPACES & VIEWING:
     * Atrium — two-story sky-lit lobby with floor-to-ceiling windows and a
       sweeping staircase; functions as the ship's hotel-style front desk
     * Viking Lounge — the ship's "living room"; bar, piano, dance floor,
       floor-to-ceiling windows; hosts daily port briefings, lock talks,
       cooking demonstrations, happy hour, and evening live music
     * Aquavit Lounge / Sun Room — bright casual lounge forward of the
       Viking Lounge; retractable floor-to-ceiling glass doors open onto
       the Aquavit Terrace
     * Aquavit Terrace — Viking's signature indoor/outdoor space at the bow,
       with al fresco seating, grills, and two rocking chairs at the very
       front facing the river
     * Sun Deck — runs the length of the ship with 360-degree views,
       shaded sitting area, loungers, umbrellas; railings/furnishings fold
       down hydraulically to clear low European bridges
     * Library corner — curated by London bookshop Heywood Hill; passenger
       book-exchange and board games nearby
     * Onboard Boutique — small shop for Viking logo wear and souvenirs

   DINING (all included in fare; no reservations; open seating):
     * The Restaurant — main dining room with floor-to-ceiling windows;
       breakfast and lunch buffet (with cooked-to-order menu items), plated
       four-course dinner; beer and wine included with lunch and dinner
     * Aquavit Terrace — casual indoor/outdoor alternative; light buffet
       breakfast and lunch, bistro-style dinner; smaller tables
     * 24-hour coffee nooks on both sides of the ship — espresso drinks,
       teas, cookies
     * Welcome Champagne, nightly cocktail hour, and regional wine tastings
     * Room service is limited (breakfast only; Explorer Suite guests only)

   OUTDOOR / RECREATION ON THE SUN DECK:
     * Walking track that loops the deck
     * Chef's organic herb garden (chefs harvest daily for on-board meals)
     * Putting green and shuffleboard
     * Solar panels running down the center of the deck

   ENRICHMENT & ENTERTAINMENT:
     * Daily port talks and destination lectures in the Viking Lounge
     * Local performers brought aboard in port (classical music,
       traditional folk performances, regional food/wine tastings)
     * Culinary demonstrations and cooking classes (e.g. strudel-making)
     * Scenic-sailing commentary during river locks and gorges
     * In-stateroom infotainment: 40"-42" flat-panel Sony TVs with
       movies-on-demand, CNBC, CNN, National Geographic, destination content

   STATEROOMS (all outside, all river-view):
     * Standard (water-level, smaller windows)
     * French Balcony (~135 sq ft, floor-to-ceiling door, no step-out)
     * Veranda stateroom (~205 sq ft, full step-out balcony)
     * Veranda Suite (~275 sq ft, separate sitting area, full veranda + French
       balcony, two flat-panel TVs)
     * Explorer Suite (~445 sq ft, wrap-around veranda with 270-degree views,
       two TVs; perks include priority embarkation, private transfers, in-
       suite breakfast, Silver Spirits beverage package, free laundry,
       welcome Champagne, daily minibar restock)
     * All staterooms: hotel-style bed with European linens, heated bathroom
       floors, anti-fog mirrors, premium bath products, bathrobe & slippers
       on request, telephone, safe, refrigerator, hairdryer, individual
       climate control, 220V and 110V (US-style) outlets, USB ports

   SERVICES & SUSTAINABILITY:
     * Complimentary ship-wide Wi-Fi
     * Laundry service (self-serve on some vessels; paid attended service
       on others)
     * Guest Services / concierge desk acts as hotel front desk
     * Cruise Manager handles excursions and shore programming
     * Elevator between Middle and Upper Decks only (NOT serving Lower Deck)
     * Eco features: energy-efficient hybrid diesel-electric engines (less
       vibration, lower emissions), solar panels on Sun Deck, organic herb
       garden, LED lighting throughout

   NOT PRESENT ON A VIKING LONGSHIP (common questions):
     * No casino, no nightclub, no theater, no production shows
     * No kids' or teen club (Viking is adults-only, 18+)
     * No pool or hot tubs (space does not permit on a river vessel)
     * No specialty / up-charge restaurants (all dining is included)
     * No pre-paid gratuity programs; tipping is at guest discretion
